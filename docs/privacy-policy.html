Privacy Policy for SpendWise

Effective Date: August 12, 2026 • Last Updated: August 12, 2026
Key Summary: SpendWise is designed with a 100% offline-first and privacy-centric architecture. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal or financial information with any third-party servers.

1. Information Collection and Storage

SpendWise operates entirely locally on your device:

  • Financial Data: All transaction amounts, categories, budget limits, and notes you enter are stored securely in a local SQLite database on your device using Android's sandboxed storage.
  • No Cloud Accounts Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal details (such as names, phone numbers, or bank account credentials) to use SpendWise.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: SpendWise does not track your location, browsing habits, or in-app behavior.

2. Data Export and Import (CSV)

SpendWise includes an optional CSV Export and Import feature:

  • When you export your data, a standard CSV file is generated and saved only to the folder you select via the Android Storage Access Framework.
  • We do not have access to your exported files or your cloud storage providers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

SpendWise does not sell or share any user data. If third-party services (such as Google Play Services or non-personalized ad networks) are enabled in future updates, they operate under Google's Privacy Policy and do not receive any of your private financial logs or transaction amounts.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can clear all transactions inside the app at any time via Settings → Clear All Transactions.
  • Uninstalling SpendWise permanently deletes all local application databases and saved preferences from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

SpendWise is a general audience utility app and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
